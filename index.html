House rules
  1. Three daily quests: Say Yes, Get Uncomfortable, Take a Chance. Answer a quest’s question to finish it.
  2. Each quest pays 100 XP. XP raises your level.
  3. Finish two of three to clear the day. Cleared days in a row make your streak. Miss a day and it starts again.
  4. The day turns over at 3 am, so a late-night entry counts for the day you lived.
  5. The tree: every quest is a leaf. A quest you also did on two or more of the six days before grows a root; any other is a branch. Every cleared day bears fruit. It grows from seed to old olive over about a season.
  6. A new scroll opens each day. File it when you have read it.
  7. The countdown runs to 31 December. Every square is a day: gold for cleared, pale for one quest.
  8. Tap ♪ for the music. Quest tracks play in order; battle tracks shuffle.

Everything is saved on this phone, in the app you opened from the home screen, and stays when you close it. It is not sent anywhere. Back up now and then, and restore the file on a new phone.
